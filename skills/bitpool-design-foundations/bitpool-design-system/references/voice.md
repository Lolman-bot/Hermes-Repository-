# Bitpool voice & copy

Full treatment with worked before/after rewrites is in the standalone brand-guide HTML (ships with the skill) (Voice & copy section). This is the working summary. For anything customer-facing — taglines, headlines, body, emails — the **bitpool-brand-guidelines** skill is the deeper authority on verbal voice; this file covers just enough to keep design copy on-brand.

## Personality

**Boldly practical and unapologetically real.** Confident, not preachy. State things; don't sell them.

## The moves

- **Two-tier sentences.** A title-line sets the idea, a payoff-line lands it. Every axiom card and pillar uses this rhythm.
  > Buildings that decide. / Not dashboards that **wait for you** — systems that act.
- **Bold cyan, never italic.** Wherever you'd italicise for emphasis, wrap the word in `<strong>` (brand cyan, weight 700). There is no `<em>`, no serif, no italic anywhere in the brand.
- **Specific over vague.** "60 billion records" not "lots of data." "$2.5M ARR by 2027" not "ambitious growth."
- **Build, don't blah.** Action verbs. Plain confidence.

## Banned words

Avoid SaaS filler: *leverage, empower, transform, revolutionize, seamless, synergy, cutting-edge, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, robust, holistic, best-in-class.* No emojis in marketing copy.

## Choosing what to bold

Bold the **one or two load-bearing words** in a line — the noun or verb that carries the idea (`buildings`, `act`, `shipping today`). Never bold whole clauses; never bold more than ~20% of a headline. If everything is cyan, nothing is.
